Gameweek

Advance once the gameweek is over. A free transfer accrues (to a maximum of five) and a Free Hit squad reverts. Until the Gameweek 1 deadline transfers are unlimited and free — the first free transfer arrives with Gameweek 2.

Chips used

The first set expires at the Gameweek 19 deadline; a fresh set arrives for Gameweek 20. This tool records what you chose - it never advises a chip.

Predicted points

Every projection in the tool comes from these numbers and nothing else — the squad optimiser, the horizon column and the transfer planner alike. A player or gameweek the table does not cover counts as zero. Copy the table from wherever you read it — KnightManagers, for instance — and paste it here. One row per player, one column per gameweek.

Team news

Paste what you have read about expected lineups — one player per line. This is how predicted-lineup information gets into the projections; the app does not fetch it, because no free source publishes it before the deadline.

Player adjustments

Override what the model assumes when you know better - expected minutes, chance of playing, or how often a player hits his defensive-contribution threshold.

Data & backup

Your squad, transfers, chips, overrides and protected list are stored in this browser and survive closing it. They are tied to this address, are not shared between browsers or machines, and are cleared if you clear site data — so export a copy if it would hurt to lose.
